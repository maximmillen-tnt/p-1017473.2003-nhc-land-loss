Readers for Wellington City Council's earthmoving cut and fill areas,
`get_wcc_cut_areas` and `get_wcc_fill_areas`. They map where subdivision
earthworks cut into and filled over the natural ground, which is the slope
modification factor an earthquake-induced slope failure susceptibility model
needs. Both docstrings record what the layers do not carry -- no cut height, no
cut angle, and no coverage outside Wellington City -- and that the cut layer is
licensed No Derivatives while the fill layer records no licence at all.
