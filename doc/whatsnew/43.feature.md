A new landslide hazard step rebuilds Greater Wellington's earthquake-induced
slope failure susceptibility zonation from the method published in Kingsbury
(1995), rather than deriving it from the council's layer, which is licensed no
derivatives. Slope angle is read at 10 m to match the terrain model the 1995
class boundaries were calibrated against, while cut angle and face height are
read at 1 m inside the Wellington City earthworks records, because a
subdivision cut face averages away at the coarser cell size. A figure puts the
rebuilt zones beside the published layer on a shared colour scale.
