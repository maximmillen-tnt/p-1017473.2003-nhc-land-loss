Readers for Wellington City Council's earthmoving cut and fill areas,
`get_wcc_cut_areas` and `get_wcc_fill_areas`. They map where subdivision
earthworks cut into and filled over the natural ground, which is the slope
modification factor an earthquake-induced slope failure susceptibility model
needs. Both docstrings record what the layers do not carry -- no cut height, no
cut angle, and no coverage outside Wellington City -- and that the cut layer is
licensed No Derivatives while the fill layer records no licence at all.

A reader for GNS Science's SLIDE geomorphic feature lines over Wellington City,
`get_gns_slide_morphology`: breaks in slope, cliffs, cut/fill lines, drainage
lines, tension cracks and the retaining walls visible from above. The docstring
records that the layer is CC BY 4.0, covers Wellington City only, and that its
retaining walls are a partial capture rather than an inventory.

A research script, `hazard/landslide/research/fig_wcc_earthworks_completeness.py`,
checks how complete the WCC earthworks record is by measuring how much of GNS's
independently mapped cut/fill line length falls within a tolerance of a WCC cut
or fill polygon, and how many WCC polygons GNS picked out in turn. It writes a
map and a CSV of both fractions at tolerances from 0 to 50 m.
